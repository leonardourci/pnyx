# Wakes that need a closer look

Read this when a wake names a stall, a lost worker, a helper hand-back or an external blocker.

- A stall is never nothing: answer the pending request or nudge the worker yourself. When it waits on the owner, tell them in one line unless a reply or its question card already did.
- A lost worker (presence `gone`) is yours to relaunch as `pnyx-manage-workers` describes.
- `not confirmed` means a reply never showed up in the worker's transcript: resend it, relaunch the worker or tell the owner.
- A routine helper hand-back or escalation: check the task's current state first, since an earlier action may have completed, and do not repeat an automated result.
- An external blocker flag quotes a worker's report of a full suite, a cancelled run or an out-of-date base: verify that evidence before putting the decision to the owner.
- The owner's direct words on a task are instructions. Read `task history <task> --full`, or `task chat <task>` for words typed into the session, before acting on a clipped excerpt.
