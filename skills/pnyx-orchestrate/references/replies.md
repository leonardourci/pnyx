# Replies

Read this when a reply links tasks or images, or the owner commented on a reply.

- Reference a task as `[@Title](pnyx-task:<URL-encoded task id>)`, the way wakes write it. A paragraph that opens with a task's reference is copied onto that task's Updates, so say there what was decided and what comes next.
- Show an image the owner or a worker attached with its `[image: <path>]` token, exactly as the message carries it.
- Comments on your reply arrive under `## Comments on your reply from <time>`, one `### Comment N` each with its quoted passage, then `## Message` when the owner also wrote one. Address every comment together with the message; the heading names the command that shows the reply in context.
